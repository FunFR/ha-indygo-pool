# Indygo Pool

Pool equipment controlled through the MyIndygo cloud: filtration pump, electrolyser and the modules that drive them.

## Filtration

**Pump Boost**:
A timed override that runs the filtration pump for a chosen number of hours, outside its program.
_Avoid_: Boost (alone), filtration boost

## Electrolyser

**Electrolyser**:
The salt cell that produces chlorine, driven by an output of the IPX module.
_Avoid_: Chlorinator, sterilor

**Electrolyser Mode**:
How the electrolyser decides its production: OFF, PERCENTAGE (fixed share) or AUTO (regulated by a redox or chlorine sensor).
_Avoid_: Regulation, production mode

**Production Setpoint**:
The share of its maximum output, from 10 to 100 %, the electrolyser produces in PERCENTAGE mode.
_Avoid_: Production, percentage, consigne

**Cover Reduction**:
The electrolyser's own division of its production by five while the pool cover is closed.
_Avoid_: Shutter mode, cover mode

**Electrolyser Boost**:
A timed override (12 or 24 hours) that forces electrolyser production regardless of its mode.
_Avoid_: Boost (alone), super-chlorination
